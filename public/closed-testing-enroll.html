Help us test the app before we open it to everyone. Your feedback will directly shape the final version.

Why We Need Testers

We're getting ready to release Milledgeville Connect publicly. Before we do, we want real people using it on real phones to catch bugs, improve the experience, and make sure everything works smoothly for the whole community.

What You'll Be Testing

  • Push notifications (traffic alerts, deals, events, messages)
  • Posting and interacting with Shoutouts, Marketplace & Lost & Found
  • Business features (if you're a local business owner)
  • Overall stability and performance on real devices

How to Join

  1. Fill out the form below with your Gmail address
  2. I’ll manually add you to the closed test in Google Play
  3. You’ll receive the testing link from Google Play and/or directly from Milledgeville Connect
  4. Install the app using the link and start testing
  5. Report any bugs or feedback you have

How to Find Your Gmail Address

Open the Gmail app → tap your profile picture in the top right. Your email address will be shown at the top. You must use a Gmail account to join Google Play closed testing.
