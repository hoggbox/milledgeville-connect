Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, email, and profile picture when you sign up
  • Posts, comments, marketplace listings, lost & found items
  • Location data (optional) for better local experience
  • Push notification token (for alerts)
  • Usage data to improve the app

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide community features (traffic alerts, marketplace, events, etc.)
  • Send relevant push notifications
  • Moderate content and prevent abuse
  • Improve the app based on usage data

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is only shared when necessary for app functionality (for example, your marketplace listing is visible to other users in Milledgeville).

4. Data Retention

We keep your account information and content as long as your account is active. Traffic alerts are automatically deleted after 8 hours. When you delete your account, we permanently remove your personal information and all associated content within 30 days, except where we are legally required to keep certain records.

5. Your Rights

You can request to access, correct, or delete your data at any time by contacting us at imhoggbox@gmail.com. You can also delete your account directly from the profile page inside the app.

6. Children’s Privacy

Milledgeville Connect is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at imhoggbox@gmail.com so we can delete it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
