D and D Trucking and Grading is a family-owned operation built from the ground up. Dusty and Duane Fountain started this company because they believed Central Georgia deserved dependable, honest land-work — no shortcuts, no excuses.

From a single lawn mower to a full fleet of equipment, we've grown by doing the work right the first time. Our neighbors trust us — and that means everything.
